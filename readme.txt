
================================
READ ME for Quake 2 Final Mod
================================

This is the modified source code of Quake 2 made for The Engineer of Lie's IT266 class in Spring of 2026

===========================
HOW TO INSTALL
===========================

Download the source code and the "magicmod" folder
Make sure you have purchased your own personal copy of Quake2 from a reputable source and pull the game folder out from wherever it may be directly onto your desktop. (this was done because I don't have the source code for the updated/remastered version and had to separate it from steam)

Two ways to open the modded game:
  Make either your own custom shortcut:   "C:\Users\hanna\Desktop\Quake 2\quake2.exe" +set game magicmod
  OR
  On game start open the console(~) and type "set game magicmod" press esc and start a new game

=====================
HOW TO PLAY (details about the mod)
=====================
Play as you would normally play quake 2 but with a sense of curiousity and whimsy. It is supposed to feel somewhat open world and in a perfect scenario would include an elaborate quest to eventually solve a puzzle and live happily ever after because of your feelings of fulfillment after having done said puzzle. Oh also you would be guided by the Koroks who would introduce you to this world and make you collect a bunch of seeds and stuff. (i.e. an escape room Breath of the Wild/Legend of Zelda style shrine quest built within a first person shooter) (check back in a couple months to see if I actually ended up doing that)

OTHERWISE the game is rather shallow and simple made for nothing more than the enjoyement of having a modded out Quake 2 inspired by one of my favorite video games. 

Help Screen - press F1

Using Console commands (~) you can see the new items and weapons that I have changed for this game
  "give" " " "Item Name Here"
    
my personal favorite: 
  "give Korok Leaf" - which pulls up what looks like a machine gun but instead blasts enemies and explosives backwards with a gust of wind

NEW ITEMS:
  "give Majora's Mask" + the key command shown in the inventory to use (b) - begins a powerup that makes you invisible to enemies for 30 seconds
  "give Guardian Shield" + (i) - Protects you from enemy fire and melee shots
  "give Triforce" + key bind () - Basically a Timed God Mode * 100
  "give Korok Seed" (also found within game) - collect the Korok Seeds to summon the Korok NPC and have a conversation
  "give Fairy" + key bind (e) - Revives the player when they are about to die

NEW WEAPONS:
  "Torch" - lights your way if held, infinite durability
  "Korok Leaf" - blows enemies back with a gust of wind, they take knockback damage, infinite durability
  "Traveller's Sword" - A simple melee weapon that hurts to get hit with, high durability
  "Master Sword" - A less simple melee weapon that hurts significantly more when hit, low durability
  "Great Thunderblade" - electrocutes and temporarily stuns enemies, medium durability
  "Throwing Spear" - A mixed range/melee weapon that can be thrown and go stabby stabby, dependent on spear quantity
  "Shiekah Bomb" - A bomb is launched and lands where it is thrown. It goes boom, (ideally after a button is pressed by the player)
  "Bow" - A ranged weapon that causes reasonable damage, but it gets the job done, infinite durability
  "Forest Dweller's Bow" - A ranged weapon that causes more damage and is generally very powerful, limited use.
  "Ancient Bow" - High Damage Arrows that are unaffected by gravity, requires special arrows.

NPC DIALOGUE: (talking to the large and oversized suspiciously strogg-like looking Korok NPCs)
  Using the console commands you can open a dialogue tree with the Koroks. You will be told how to do so once you have collected enough Korok Seeds. 
  If you don't feel like going on that quest you can simply type into the console(~): "startconvo" and follow along as dialogue options are shown.
